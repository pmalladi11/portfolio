Projects

TSA Robotics: 4WD Robot with 4-Joint Arm

Technology Student Association · Oct 2025 – Present

A remote-controlled pick-and-place robot taken through three complete redesigns. Went from a relay-switched prototype too heavy to turn to a compact motor-shield drivetrain with a modified RoArm M2-S arm, controlled over Wi-Fi from a browser joystick and an Xbox controller.

  • Arduino Uno R4
  • C++
  • Python
  • ESP32
  • Motor control
  • 3D printing

Science Olympiad Electric Vehicle

Science Olympiad · Sep 2025 – Mar 2026

A distance-targeting electric vehicle built around an Arduino, a BTS7960 H-bridge, and a 550 brushed motor. I designed a custom Arduino shield to replace the breadboard and built an on-vehicle interface (LCD, buttons, potentiometer) to set the target distance at the track.

  • Arduino Uno R3
  • C++
  • Custom shield
  • H-bridge
  • I2C

TSA Drone Challenge: 5-inch FPV Drone

Technology Student Association · Oct 2025 – Present

A 5-inch FPV quadcopter built from components: soldered 6S motors to a 4-in-1 ESC, wired an ELRS receiver over UART, integrated an analog video system, and configured the flight controller in Betaflight. Includes airspace research for flying at the national conference.

  • Betaflight
  • Soldering
  • UART / CRSF
  • Brushless ESC
  • FAA rules
